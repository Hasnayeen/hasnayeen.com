House Builder — design a 3D house in your browser

House Builder is a free browser tool for designing houses in 3D. Draw floors and walls on a plot, add doors, windows, stairs and roofs, furnish rooms and set up lights. Watch your house in daylight, golden hour or at night, record a cinematic tour, and export dimensioned floor plans as PDF, SVG or PNG.
